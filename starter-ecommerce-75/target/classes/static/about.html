Developia Kids

Developia Kids in Baku is an excellent place for children to start their journey into the world of technology. With engaging and professional instructors, kids learn programming through fun and interactive lessons using languages like Scratch and Java. The academy offers both online and in-person options, making it flexible for families.
Parents love the structured curriculum, the friendly environment, and the noticeable progress their children make. Many students gain confidence, creativity, and valuable tech skills — all while having fun. Highly recommended for young future developers!
